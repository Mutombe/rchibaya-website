Expert answers

Questions clients often ask

Straight answers to common questions.

What happens at a first consultation?

We listen, review your documents and set out your options, likely timelines and fees. Everything you tell us stays confidential.

Do you advise foreign investors?

Yes. We handle structuring, company formation, joint ventures, financing and exchange control, and the approvals you need.

Can you handle property transfers?

Our notaries and conveyancers manage transfers, mortgage bonds and notarial instruments from instruction to registration.

Do you help with wills and estates?

We draft wills, plan succession and administer estates, and we guide families through inheritance disputes with care.

How soon will you reply?

Within one business day. For urgent matters, call reception on (0242) 703 505/6 during office hours.
